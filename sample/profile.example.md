## Candidate Profile Summary

Early-career data and business analyst. Best-fit roles sit around analytics, business intelligence, data-driven product/operations, and reporting & insight work, with a clear emphasis on turning data into decisions for non-technical stakeholders. Use this profile only for fast first-pass shortlisting: reject hard exclusions, save plausible jobs, and leave detailed fit scoring to the later evaluation workflow.

## Hard Exclusions

If any of these are true, skip the job immediately, regardless of other signals:

- **Company:** Big 4 (Deloitte, PwC, EY, KPMG), MBB (McKinsey, BCG, Bain)
- **Seniority (title or scope):** Head, Director, VP, C-level, Chief, Partner. Also skip Manager / Senior Manager / Principal / Lead / Architect / Staff / Senior Engineer only when paired with people management, P&L ownership, or 5+ YoE requirement. Any seniority stays at 1-3 YoE stays in regardless the title.
- **Experience floor:** Job requires 5+ years of relevant experience as a hard minimum. "3+ years" is acceptable; "5+ years" is not.
- **Country:** Any job from the following countries is automatically excluded: Bulgaria, Croatia, Cyprus, Estonia, Greece, Italy, Latvia, Lithuania, Malta, Poland, Portugal, Romania, Slovakia, Slovenia.
- **Obvious non-match:** No meaningful AI angle in the role itself.
- **Language:** Requires, strongly prefers, or primarily operates in any language other than English. Skip if the job description itself is mostly non-English, if the role requirements mention non-English language requirement.
- **Others:** Internship, already applied, unpaid or paid by hours, suspicious/spammy, location is region-level remote (country-level remote is fine), fixed term or contractor work or anything that indicates it is not a full-time job, clear indication of no visa sponsorship.
